Edge Architecture: Mobile Offline Resilience vs. Safety-Critical Boundaries
[CP STANCE] SAFETY-CRITICAL STATE 
Strictly CP Consistency & Partition Tolerance): Block approvals 
& line clearances NEVER proceed on optimistic eventual 
consistency. If network partition occurs, the system fails closed.
[AP STANCE] DASHBOARD / ANALYTICS VIEWS 
AP Availability & Partition Tolerance): Live operational telemetry 
degrades gracefully to eventual consistency with transparent lag 
counters.
TIER 1: INFORMATIONAL TASKS 
CAN be queued offline
Queued in local encrypted SQLite until cloud connection is 
restored:
● Site arrival confirmation & progress logging
● Photo evidence capture
● Equipment inspection checklists & field notes queue
TIER 2: SAFETY-CRITICAL TASKS 
STRICTLY FORBIDDEN OFFLINE
Regulated Operations: Permit-to-Work issuance, 25kV Catenary 
Re-energization sign-off, and Line Block Cancellation.
Requirements: MUST have verified end-to-end cloud 
connectivity, active session authentication, and multi-party 
cryptographic confirmation. Offline bypass of safety controls is 
strictly prohibited.
